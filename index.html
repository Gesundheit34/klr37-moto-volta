About This Adventure

The Kawasaki KLR650 has been an icon of adventure riding since 1987. After 36 years of proven reliability and countless journeys, we're gathering the moto community around Athens to celebrate this legendary bike.

Join us for the Moto-Volta – a scenic loop through the Athens region where KLR owners and adventure riders unite to share the road, stories, and the spirit of exploration.
